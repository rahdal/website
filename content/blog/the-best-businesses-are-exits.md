---
title: "The Best Businesses are Exits"
date: "September 21, 2024"
description: "Important products are exits from the traditional, outdated ways of doing thing"
---

The best businesses are exits from the traditional, outdated ways of doing things.

When you look at the most successful products and companies, they often represent a significant departure from established methods.

Tesla isn't just a car company; it's an exit from fossil fuel-powered transportation.

Airbnb isn't just a hospitality company; it's an exit from traditional hotel accommodations.

Stripe isn't just a payment processor; it's an exit from complex, developer-unfriendly financial infrastructure.

The most valuable innovations don't just improve existing systems incrementally—they provide complete exits from them.

When evaluating business ideas, ask yourself: "Is this an exit from something people desperately want to leave behind?"
