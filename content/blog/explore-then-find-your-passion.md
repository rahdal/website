---
title: "Explore. Then Find Your Passion."
date: "August 31, 2024"
description: "Following your passion is impossible if you've never explored"
---

"Follow your passion" is perhaps the most common career advice given to young people. But there's a fundamental problem with this advice: how can you follow your passion if you don't know what it is yet?

The truth is, passion isn't something you're born with—it's something you discover through exploration and experience.

Before you can find your passion, you need to explore. You need to try different things, experiment with various fields, and expose yourself to diverse experiences.

Many successful people didn't start with a clear passion. They started by being curious, by saying yes to opportunities, and by being willing to learn.

Steve Jobs didn't start with a passion for technology. He explored calligraphy, Eastern philosophy, and various other interests before eventually finding his way to technology.

J.K. Rowling didn't always know she wanted to be a writer. She explored different careers and life experiences before Harry Potter came to her.

The path to finding your passion isn't straight—it's winding, with many detours and unexpected discoveries along the way.

So instead of pressuring yourself to find your one true passion right away, focus on exploring. Try new things, develop skills, meet new people, and remain open to possibilities.

Your passion will reveal itself through this process of exploration and growth.
