This summer ended up being pretty chaotic

The first week at Palantir is "indoc" (short for indoctrination), which is basically onboarding but they make it confusing on purpose to prepare you for the Palantir culture. Karp (CEO) and Shyam (CTO) both had AMAs with the interns which were pretty interesting/allowed you to understand their vision for the company. Something that seemed cool but ended up being lowk hell was the indoc hackathon. The idea seemed fun: you spend time using foundry and building something, but a lot of teams (including mine) ended up grinding the project. I personally could not be bothered to grind so I left at like 7pm when my team stayed until like 11pm on one of the nights. My teammates were great though, very cool people. I personally hated this entire week as I wasn't used to the hours (9-6) and also was feeling ill.

Towards the end of the first week I found out that I would be working on hospitals. Going into the internship, hospitals was my top choice team, but I started to talk to full timers and realized that I might be fucked. Basically, hospitals has the reputation of being the grindiest team at Palantir, and Palantir is already pretty grindy.

I walked into work the next Monday and met Anshul (TL) and James (Echo) who comprised the entirety of my team. Anshul is like a fratty fob and James is this regular asian dude. Anyways, when I first talked to them they asked me if I was here this weekend because they thought they saw me. Immediately I was like ??? because 1. why tf would I be at the office on a weekend 2. why are y'all at the office on a weekend. After talking to them more I basically realized that this dude saw Shourya in the bathroom (this mf doesn't even work here) and thought it was me. I find a desk and start making conversation with them and learning about more about the team. The convo lowk was hella mid. I didn't really click immediately with either of them. I also asked James when they leave the office and this dude says 9pm and I was like 😳.

I started working and it was pretty chill. I did some EDI file generation stuff (lowk similar to c1 which gave me PTSD) and was learning more about foundry. I didn't expect that I would living in Foundry as an FDE. Prior to joining, I was kinda thinking that I would be building more custom stuff for the customer and not solely work in Foundry. FDEs do work a lot in Foundry, but being on a pilot kinda excarbates this cuz we have to go from 0->1 on everything. Over these first two months I was getting glazed pretty hard by Anshul and he was trying to get me to either skip my masters or take the fall semester off. Bro was talking about some "I don't see a ceiling for you" and "I haven't asked any other intern to do this". I ultimately decided against staying at Palantir for a longer period of time, mainly because I value college and my senior year a lot. A lot of the people that do fade school to work at Palantir are orthodox jews people who believe that their school is failing them, so it makes more sense for them.

I continued to do work in a lot of different areas of foundry and also spent time demoing to customers and collecting feedback. I didn't enjoy some of the no-code work that much and expressed that to my lead (you have a separate lead at palantir who isn't on your team).

Around this time, I was debugging some of my code and needed to ask for advice in slack. I posted a screenshot in one of our company-wide slack channels (~1k members). I didn't think about it that much until I was talking to Anshul around 30 minutes later and he just goes "delete that shit immediately". Turns out I had posted PHI (Protected Health Information) in the slack 😬. Turns out that posting health information in the company slack is not good. We had to inform legal and the data protection team that night, and they told us that there was a chance that we would have to inform the client tomorrow. Luckily, there was nothing important happening tomorrow, right? Haha yeah we were actually having contract negotiations with the client tomorrow, so I was genuinely tweaking as I went to bed. To my relief, since the data leak was miniscule, we didn't have to inform the client and my job was saved.

A little bit later I noticed a change in James' and, to a degree, Anshul's behavior. James started working from home a lot and also just looked depressed whenever he was at the office. He genuinely looked like he wanted to shoot himself. We also just like wouldn't talk at all over these next few weeks. I didn't look too much into it and just assumed he was out of it or burned out. Anshul also didn't talk much and spent less time at his desk. Then, at around 10 am on a Thursday, James walks up to me, shakes my hand, says "I just got fired", and walks out of the office. I was pretty taken aback by it initially. I think someone being fired is something you know happens but don't really expect to see, especially during an internship.

LLJ 🕊️

After that I think the vibes of the team were pretty fucked. Someone else on Hospitals also got fired that seem week, which didn't help. Contract extension negotiations were heating up, so Anshul was busy with that, and I was kinda bouncing around various tasks. I spent an insane amount of time sifting through data and tables trying to understand how the client's business worked, but I ended with more questions than answers. The contract ended up getting extended, which was a nice win for the team. We onboarded two new people (Shoutout M16Z, DDS legend), and then Anshul had a personal emergency and left. That basically brings us to now, where I'm gone and everyone on the team is a fresh face.

Oh also this mf Anshul lost one of my airpods. He accidentally took my case home with him, and when I reclaimed them, the left airpod was missing. FindMy said that it was in the office, but I couldn't find it.
